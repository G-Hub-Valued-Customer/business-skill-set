# GROUP CLM: CLAIMS, CREDIT AND THE COUNTERFACTUAL DENOMINATOR

### SD-CLM-01 The permanent record principle
- Rule: "THIS OUTPUT BECOMES A PERMANENT RECORD. Every rule that constrains a
  claim exists because an overclaim, a misattributed peer result, or an invented
  figure can damage a career. When a rule and convenience conflict, the rule
  wins."
- Prevents: rule erosion under time or convenience pressure.
- Source: R-D0
- Applies: REVIEW, PLANNING, SCORECARD

### SD-CLM-02 A results list is a set of true, quantified, orphaned facts
- Rule: the problem the method exists to solve. A true number "does not say what
  action produced it, whether it beat anyone, or why the organization should care.
  The reader supplies the context, and in a calibration room nobody does." And:
  "People are already doing the work. They cannot phrase it."
- Source: R Mission
- Applies: REVIEW, PLANNING, SCORECARD

### SD-CLM-03 Credit attaches only to the units of the business you are accountable for
- Rule: credit only for the performance of a creditable entity. No credit for the
  movement of the wider population it sits in.
- Mechanism: an explicit allowlist of creditable entities plus an explicit
  denylist of the totals, aggregates and outside parties that are never claimable.
- General form of the denylist: you may not claim the movement of any population
  you did not choose the composition of. That covers wider-market growth, a
  tailwind, a price change made above you, an inherited agreement and a macro
  effect, without naming any of them.
- Source: R-D1
- Applies: REVIEW, PLANNING, SCORECARD

### SD-CLM-04 Every number carries a second number the writer did not choose
- Rule: this is the load-bearing generalization of the credit doctrine. A category
  is one instance of that second number. The general name for the slot is the
  COUNTERFACTUAL DENOMINATOR: the figure that answers "compared to what, and how
  much of that was you."
- Mechanism: the ranked ladder, best first.
  1. Share of a bounded addressable population. Best. Any business with a bounded
     addressable set has this even without an outside competitor.
  2. A matched control group inside the business. The untouched, unassigned or
     later-treated segment. Strongest available where there is no external
     market: the same environment, minus the writer.
  3. The peer distribution. Where there is no external market to move underneath
     everyone, rank becomes a legitimate primary proof rather than a supplement,
     subject to the tie-count rule and to BOTH floors of SD-SPN-16: the peer set has
     to be large enough to name safely and large enough to derive a band from, and
     those are two conditions and not one.
  4. Plan or commitment attainment. Percent of a target set before the period by
     someone other than the writer. Weaker because the target is negotiable, but
     already a first-class claim: attainment above target is one of the strongest
     claims a person can make.
  5. Own prior-period run rate. Weakest of the numeric benchmarks, because it
     controls for nothing external, and it is exactly the claim the
     ground-moved failure punishes. Carry the warning with it.
  6. A bare denominator population. Not a comparison, but a bound on magnitude.
     The floor: no number ships alone.
- Rule: the output records which rung each claim used, and the rung determines how
  strongly the claim may be worded.
- Source: R central portability question; R-D34; R gate G1
- Applies: REVIEW, PLANNING, SCORECARD

### SD-CLM-05 Every business must name what its ground is
- Rule: a business with no external market is not a business without a benchmark.
  It is a business whose benchmark is internal. But the specific failure the
  ground-moved warning exists to deliver, that a large positive number can still
  be a miss because the ground moved under it, arrives in such a business as a
  rising baseline rather than as a rising market. The binding must therefore force
  the business to name what its ground is, and must refuse to let a claim ship at
  achievement strength until that answer is bound.
- Mechanism: MOVING_GROUND_NAME and MOVING_GROUND_SOURCE, required.
- Source: R central portability question
- Applies: REVIEW, PLANNING, SCORECARD

### SD-CLM-06 Two comparative quantities, and never interchange them
- Rule: a RATE DELTA is entity growth minus benchmark growth, in percentage points
  of growth. A PROPORTION DELTA is end share minus start share, in share points,
  where share is entity value divided by benchmark value. They are not the same
  and they have different units. "Confusing them produces overclaims of several
  multiples."
- Mechanism: EVERY EMITTED CLAIM MUST NAME ITS UNIT. Never write a bare "points".
- Prevents: "A reviewer who reads share points as growth points sees a claim
  several times larger than the truth."
- Note: the never-interchange rule and the mandatory unit naming survive any
  rebinding verbatim and are the single most valuable pair to preserve exactly.
- Source: R-D2
- Applies: REVIEW, PLANNING, SCORECARD

### SD-CLM-07 Classify by formula, not by direction table
- Rule: label equals WIN when the rate delta exceeds plus DEADBAND, MISS when it
  falls below minus DEADBAND, FLAT when its absolute value is at or under
  DEADBAND. When the benchmark denominator is zero, negative or absent, the label
  is UNPAIRED and the entity routes to execution claiming with a denominator
  population.
- Rule: "Benchmark growth is never itself a label. An entity that grew 27 percent
  while its benchmark grew 32 percent is a MISS at minus 5.0 percentage points of
  growth, regardless of how large 27 percent looks."
- Prevents: the environment-credit trap, "the most seductive error in this data.
  It reads as a triumph and it is a miss."
- Source: R-D3
- Applies: REVIEW, PLANNING, SCORECARD

### SD-CLM-08 Every claim carries four parts, in order
- Rule: ACTION, what the person did, inferred from evidence and confirmed by the
  person. RESULT, the claimable number with its unit named. PROOF, the
  counterfactual denominator or a peer rank with its tie count. CONSEQUENCE, the
  named organizational mechanism it advances.
- Rule: "The ACTION half is what people omit. It is inferable and it is the reason
  this method exists."
- Source: R-D4
- Applies: REVIEW
 
### SD-CLM-09 Resolve every claim as high as the evidence defends, and cite the highest level reached
- Rule: rank by altitude reached, then peer rank, then magnitude. "A top-rung
  claim leads. A bottom-rung claim is a footnote. Never lead with a footnote."
- Source: R-D5
- Applies: REVIEW, PLANNING

### SD-CLM-10 A claim may ladder no higher than the level the person can credibly influence
- Rule: citing above the ceiling reads as inflation. Bind an altitude ceiling per
  role.
- Mechanism, and the reason it works: "ENFORCEMENT IS LEXICAL, NOT JUDGMENT. When
  a claim cites a level ABOVE the role's own scope, the consequence clause must
  use a contribution verb and must NOT use an ownership verb." Contributing to a
  goal is legitimate for a front-line person; delivering it is not. The gate tests
  the verb lists, "which makes the ceiling mechanically checkable rather than a
  matter of taste."
- Source: R-D5
- Applies: REVIEW

### SD-CLM-34 The noise band is PER RUNG, and plan attainment carries one that needs no peers
- Rule: the FLAT band is a MAPPING from counterfactual rung to a band with its
  UNIT NAMED, never a single scalar. The unit of the delta differs by rung:
  percentage points of SHARE on rung 1, percentage points of the treated-minus-
  control GAP on rung 2, percentage points of GROWTH on rungs 3 and 5, and PERCENT
  OF TARGET on rung 4. A scalar defined as being "in the unit of that delta" names
  four different quantities depending on which rung read it, and a band read in the
  wrong unit either labels noise as a result or silences a real one.
- **Rule: THE UNITS ABOVE ARE THE UNITS OF A MOVEMENT, AND A LEVEL COMPARED AGAINST
  A LEVEL IS A FOURTH UNIT.** Comparing one rate LEVEL against another, a retention
  rate against a comparator median retention rate, is the commonest comparison in a
  service business, and its delta is in PERCENTAGE POINTS OF THE MEASURE'S OWN RATE.
  It is not share points, not growth points and not percent of target, so under a
  three-unit mapping no band existed for it, and with no band the failsafe below
  silenced the label. The result was that the three figures a reader most wants
  labelled shipped with a raw delta and nothing else.
- Rule: the level-delta band is DATA-DERIVED from the comparator set's own spread,
  in the same way a peer-distribution band is, and it is subject to every rule that
  governs a comparator set: it is not emitted where the set is below EITHER floor of
  SD-SPN-16, and it is not emitted where the set was built by the writer, per
  SD-CLM-30. **The floor that binds a BAND is MIN_POPULATION_FOR_NORM**, because a band
  derived from a spread needs the spread to be a distribution; ANONYMITY_FLOOR binds
  whether the set may be described at all. Naming only the anonymity floor here is what
  let a walk deliver a set that could be named and could not be banded, so this band
  was computed and then withheld on every run at the documented defaults. The exact
  derivation lives in the skill that computes it.
- Rule: a level delta and a movement delta are NEVER banded against each other's
  band, and both are never emitted as though they were the same finding. A rate that
  is flat in movement and below the comparator level is two facts, not one.
- Rule: rung 6, the bare denominator, has NO band, because a bare count carries no
  delta to band. That is a stated absence rather than a missing entry.
- **Rule: RUNG 4 HAS A FIXED BAND IN PERCENT OF TARGET AND IS THEREFORE ALWAYS
  COMPUTABLE. IT NEVER DEPENDS ON A PEER SPREAD.** This is the half that was
  missing, and its absence produced the wrong silence at the top of the
  organization: the most senior reader has no peers, so no peer-derived band could
  be computed, so NO figure of theirs carried a label at all. Their headline
  attainment and their worst failure both shipped unlabelled. Plan attainment does
  not need to know how anybody else did in order to say that a figure well short of
  target is short: the target IS the comparison, and it was set by somebody else
  before the period.
- Mechanism: on rung 4 the delta is attainment minus 100 percent of target, the
  band is a percent of target, and the label follows directly. A reader with no
  peers still gets a labelled figure wherever a plan exists.
- Rule: the band and its UNIT are printed beside every labelled figure, so a reader
  can see what size of movement was treated as noise on the rung that figure used.
- Rule: where a rung's band genuinely cannot be computed, that rung emits no label
  and prints the raw delta with its unit. That failsafe is unchanged; what changes
  is that it no longer fires on rung 4, where it was never necessary.
- Prevents: a band applied in the wrong unit, and a senior reader receiving a page
  of unlabelled numbers because the mechanism for labelling them was tied to peers
  they do not have.
- Source: review acceptance run, D4 and D5
- Applies: PLANNING, REVIEW, SCORECARD

### SD-CLM-11 Tie handling is mandatory
- Rule: "When the person's rank is shared, the claim must say so. Tied best in
  scope is honest. Led the scope is an overclaim when six peers matched the
  result. Compute the count of peers at the same value before writing any
  superlative. A superlative a reviewer can disprove in one query destroys the
  credibility of every other claim in the document."
- Source: R-D26
- Applies: REVIEW, PLANNING, SCORECARD

### SD-CLM-12 No named third parties, including by indirect identification
- Rule: peer comparison is anonymous and positional. The rule also covers indirect
  identification: in a small peer set a positional claim can name someone by
  elimination, and a compound identifier resolves to one person. Below
  ANONYMITY_FLOOR, and equally below MIN_POPULATION_FOR_NORM where a comparison is
  what would be drawn, generalize rather than rank and take the procedure in
  SD-SPN-16 rather than deciding locally what generalizing means. Behavioural examples
  describe
  behaviour without naming the colleague involved, "since those examples often
  touch a third party's personal circumstances and travel into a record that
  person cannot see."
- Source: R-D35
- Applies: REVIEW, PLANNING, SCORECARD

### SD-CLM-13 Work performed by a direct report is team credit, never first-person action
- Rule: where the evidence identifies WHO performed the work, tag it. "A leader
  whose record claims personal execution of a subordinate's work has an integrity
  problem if it is ever checked against the logs."
- General form: the credit doctrine applied to the vertical axis rather than the
  market axis.
- Source: R-D28
- Applies: REVIEW, PLANNING, SCORECARD

### SD-CLM-14 Show the benchmark, never claim it
- Rule: every benchmark present in the data appears in the output, either as the
  comparison attached to a claim or as the denominator of a proportion, and never
  as the subject of an achievement sentence. "The subject of the sentence
  decides. If the subject is not something the writer is accountable for, the
  sentence does not belong in the body."
- Companion: a benchmark whose metric is unresolved or unpaired appears only in
  the verification list and never in the body, since its number is not
  trustworthy. Both placements satisfy the visibility requirement. "Nothing is
  dropped for being unflattering."
- Source: R-D34
- Applies: REVIEW, PLANNING, SCORECARD

### SD-CLM-15 Test the entity the number describes, not the grammatical subject, and test per number
- Rule: apply the claim-subject test to the MEASURED ENTITY of each number. "A
  sentence carrying two numbers with two different measured entities is tested
  twice and fails if either fails." Action-first phrasing is house style and must
  not be penalized.
- Source: R gate G1
- Applies: REVIEW
 
### SD-CLM-16 A rank never substitutes for the counterfactual denominator where an external environment exists
- Rule: where the environment can move underneath everyone, a peer rank is
  supplemental and never a substitute, "because an entity can rank first among
  peers while lagging its benchmark, which is a MISS." Where there is no such
  shared environment, the peer distribution IS the primary control. Which case
  applies is a binding, not a judgment.
- Source: R gate G1; R central portability question
- Applies: REVIEW, PLANNING, SCORECARD

### SD-CLM-17 Under-finding wins is the most common failure of a self-written record
- Rule: "One total number is not a performance review. The objective is to isolate
  every defensible win the data supports across the person's whole span of
  control, then let them choose."
- Mechanism: enumerate a CUT LATTICE over the dimensions of the person's
  accountability, and run every cut the role and the data permit. The dimensions
  that generalize: the self-determined subset as a bloc; each managed grouping as
  a bloc compared to the same grouping outside the writer's scope; the top
  individual units by contribution; breadth and depth of what was delivered;
  upgrades to terms, tiers or programme participation; process discipline against
  the eligible population; discretionary engagement; and at the aggregate tier,
  whole-scope performance against the next level up, each top account rolled, each
  entity rolled, team and system outcomes including capability installed, and the
  distribution of performance inside the scope.
- Rule: the distribution cut belongs only to leaders and is worth preserving
  verbatim: "A leader who lifted the bottom of the distribution has a real win
  that no single-unit number shows."
- Rule: rank candidates by altitude, then span-of-control strength, then peer
  rank, then magnitude. An impossible cut is recorded as unavailable and never
  halts.
- Source: R-D9
- Applies: REVIEW, PLANNING, SCORECARD

### SD-CLM-18 Present findings as a working list the person edits, and drop means drop
- Rule: the candidate list is presented "as a working list the user edits, not as
  a finding they receive." They may add, modify or drop. "This is the cheapest
  possible moment to correct the analysis. A user who drops two wins here is
  exercising judgment the method does not have." Dropped items leave the candidate
  list entirely and are never carried into a closing section as consolation.
- Source: R-D9
- Applies: REVIEW, PLANNING, SCORECARD

### SD-CLM-19 Reconstruct rather than halt when the person has no documentation
- Rule: where no objectives were supplied and the person chooses reconstruction,
  cluster the evidence by the goal it discharges, require at least
  SYNTHESIS_MIN_EVIDENCE items per cluster, cap at SYNTHESIS_MAX_OBJECTIVES by
  merging the weakest clusters into the nearest neighbour rather than emitting one
  more, write each at the person's altitude ceiling, derive measures from the
  quantified outcomes inside the cluster, and attach the evidence citations for
  review.
- Rule: "most people cannot fill a blank field but can correct a draft containing
  their real work." This path is the product and must not be blocked by any data
  gate. It never halts: with no cluster of two, emit a single objective from the
  person's own answer, flagged as reconstructed.
- Source: R-D10
- Applies: REVIEW

### SD-CLM-20 Never silently backdate, and never characterize a reconstruction as contemporaneous
- Rule: an objective derived after the fact has no true start or due date. Emit
  both as explicitly reconstructed and awaiting confirmation, flag the objective
  as a reconstruction, and tell the person plainly before emit.
- Rule: a dated comment states the date the WORK occurred. It does not assert when
  the entry was written, and the method never characterizes reconstructed entries
  as contemporaneous logging. "Where the host system timestamps entry creation,
  that timestamp is the truth about authorship and this method does not attempt to
  shape the impression it gives."
- Source: R-D10; R-D32
- Applies: REVIEW

### SD-CLM-21 Never infer a target to manufacture a completion percentage
- Rule: a measure with no stated target cannot produce a completion percentage.
  Mark it ungradable, ask, and route the phrasing for rewrite. "NEVER invent a
  retrospective target to manufacture completion math on an objective that never
  carried one." Choosing "no target was set for this" is honest and acceptable.
- Companion: values above 100 percent are legitimate and are stated as
  achieved-over-target, never clipped.
- Source: R-D31
- Applies: REVIEW, PLANNING, SCORECARD

### SD-CLM-22 Activity is not claimable
- Rule: four failure patterns are flagged automatically: no metric, no deadline,
  no population, and activity rather than outcome. "The last is the most common
  and most damaging."
- Mechanism: the enforced shape is verb plus metric plus population plus deadline.
- Source: R-D33
- Applies: REVIEW, PLANNING, SCORECARD

### SD-CLM-23 Visible coaching, not silent correction
- Rule: show the original, the rewrite, and a one-line reason. Never suppress an
  item because it failed grading; emit it unscored and note that it could not be
  graded.
- Source: R-D33
- Applies: REVIEW, SCORECARD

### SD-CLM-24 An illustrative number in a template is never a value
- Rule: "Every figure, population and date in a rewrite the user accepts must trace
  to the ledger, an answer, or the data. Anything unsourced is emitted as a
  bracketed placeholder. A rewrite seeded with an invented target becomes the
  standard the person is graded against next period."
- Source: R-D33
- Applies: REVIEW, PLANNING, SCORECARD

### SD-CLM-25 Learning is synthesized from losses, and it names the mechanism
- Rule: for every MISS, diagnose from the data, then propose a transferable
  learning that ladders to a stated goal. "A learning is not I need to do better.
  It names the mechanism."
- Mechanism: isolate which lever failed by comparing the person's rank across
  related process measures. Coverage complete but the outcome falling points away
  from frequency and toward depth or breadth.
- General form: compare your rank across related process measures to isolate the
  failed lever.
- Source: R-D29
- Applies: REVIEW, SCORECARD

### SD-CLM-26 Show every benchmark, keep every loss
- Rule: nothing is dropped from the output for being unflattering.
- Source: R gate G7

### SD-CLM-27 THE COLD START. Governing.
- Rule: a COLD START is a run in which the population being scored has no prior
  period of its own. It is a first-class operating condition, not an arithmetic
  edge case, and it is governed by this rule and the six that follow. Where this
  group and any other instruction disagree about a cold population, this group
  governs.
- Two kinds, and they are handled differently. They must never be conflated:
  - A COLD UNIT INSIDE A WARM POPULATION. One unit is new; the population around
    it has history. The existing rules already handle this correctly: the unit is
    included at the floor of the measure, ranks last, is visible there rather than
    dropped, and its period-over-period change is left blank rather than infinite.
    Nothing in this group changes that.
  - A COLD POPULATION. No unit, or almost no unit, has a prior period. Every
    comparison the method would normally draw is either undefined or is drawn
    against something the writer's own team is currently building. This is the
    case the bundle did not have a rule for, and it is the case where a confident
    number arrives through the front door.
- The test that separates them: the share of the in-scope population that has a
  prior period. Above COLD_START_MIN_PRIOR_SHARE the population is WARM and any
  unit below it is a cold unit. At or below it the POPULATION is cold.
- Rule: a cold population is a normal thing for a business to have. A launch, a
  new line, a new market, a new site, an acquisition still on its own systems, a
  person who took over a book last quarter. The method's job is to say true things
  about it, not to refuse.
- Source: report 2 D5
- Applies: PLANNING, REVIEW, SCORECARD

### SD-CLM-28 A cold start is DETECTED, never declared
- Rule: the run detects a cold population from the data. It does not wait for the
  user to say so, does not ask, and does not rely on a binding. A user who is
  living inside a launch does not think of themselves as an edge case and will
  not volunteer it, and an executive answering a binding interview will answer for
  the mature business because that is the business they run.
- Mechanism, three tests, any one of which marks the population COLD. All three
  run on every run, before scoring:
  1. THE HISTORY TEST, AND IT IS ONLY EVALUABLE WHERE A PRIOR-PERIOD COLUMN
     RESOLVED. The share of in-scope units carrying a usable prior-period value is
     at or below COLD_START_MIN_PRIOR_SHARE. Where NO prior-period column resolved
     at all, the share is UNDEFINED, not zero, and this test DOES NOT FIRE and
     DOES NOT PASS: it is recorded as NOT EVALUABLE, with the reason. A file that
     carries no history column is the ordinary shape of a single-snapshot export,
     which is what almost every first run is handed, and reading its absence as
     evidence of a young population would mark nearly every first run COLD.
     Absence of a column is a fact about the export. It is not a fact about the
     business.
  2. THE ENTRY TEST. No in-scope unit has an entry date before the current
     window opened, or the earliest entry date found sits inside the current or
     immediately preceding window.
  3. THE UNIFORMITY TEST. The ranking measure is zero, blank or identical across
     the whole in-scope population, which is what a population with no history
     looks like when it has no entry date to read either.
- Rule: record which test fired, and the figure that fired it, in the audit and in
  the front panel. A detection nobody can see is a guess with better manners.
  Record also every test that was NOT EVALUABLE, and why, because a reader
  cannot weigh a detection without knowing how many of the three tests could
  actually speak.
- **THE CORROBORATION RULE. A SINGLE FIRING TEST THAT ANOTHER EVALUABLE TEST
  FLATLY CONTRADICTS DOES NOT MARK THE POPULATION COLD ON ITS OWN.** Where exactly
  one test fires and a different test that WAS evaluable points the other way, the
  population is recorded as COLD-CONTESTED rather than COLD. The contradiction is
  named on the front panel with both figures, the cold-start claim restrictions of
  SD-CLM-31 and SD-CLM-32 still apply because the safe reading is the restrictive
  one, but the rung re-evaluation of SD-CLM-29 does NOT strike a rung whose only
  ground for striking is the contested test. The worked contradiction, neutral: a
  file with no prior-period column and an earliest entry date two decades before
  the window. The entry test is evidence of a long-established population and it
  is answerable from the data; nothing about the missing column outranks it.
- Rule: where two or more tests fire, or where the only firing test is
  uncontradicted by any evaluable test, the population is COLD, full stop, and no
  corroboration is required.
- Rule: detection is scoped to the population being scored, per SD-POP-23. A
  business may be warm in one population and cold in another on the same morning,
  and usually is.
- Prevents: the whole failure resting on a user volunteering the one fact they are
  least likely to think of.
- Source: report 2 D5
- Applies: PLANNING, REVIEW, SCORECARD

### SD-CLM-29 Rung availability is scoped to the population and is RE-EVALUATED, never inherited
- Rule: the counterfactual rungs a business can produce are a property of a
  population, not of a company. A cold population does not inherit the rung
  availability of the mature business around it. On detection of a cold start, the
  available rungs are RE-EVALUATED for that population alone.
- Mechanism: rung availability is bindable per population or per segment as well
  as organization-wide. Where a population-scoped binding exists, it governs for
  that population. Where none exists and the population is detected COLD, the
  organization-wide list is not inherited: each rung is tested against the cold
  population on its own terms and struck where it fails.
- The tests, per rung, against a cold population:
  - Share of an addressable set: struck unless the addressable set is bounded by
    something outside the writer's own action. See SD-CLM-30.
  - Matched control: available only if a genuine untreated segment exists inside
    the cold population itself. A control drawn from the mature business is not a
    control for the launch.
  - Peer distribution: available only if the peers are also cold. Ranking a cold
    unit against warm peers measures age, not performance.
  - Plan attainment: normally AVAILABLE, and usually the strongest rung a cold
    population has, provided the plan was set before the period by somebody other
    than the writer.
  - Own prior run rate: struck by definition. There is no prior run rate.
  - Bare denominator: always available. It is the floor and it is never struck.
- Rule: name every rung struck, and why, beside the figures it would have carried.
- This is the structural cause of the defect: a single organization-wide list of
  available rungs, with no way to say that a part of the business cannot produce
  one of them.
- Source: report 2 D5 and D1
- Applies: PLANNING, REVIEW, SCORECARD

### SD-CLM-30 A denominator the writer is building is not a counterfactual
- Rule: a comparison is only a comparison when the second number was not chosen,
  and is not being created, by the person making the claim. Where the denominator
  of a share is itself under construction by the work being claimed, that share is
  not a counterfactual. It is the numerator wearing a second name.
- Rule: where the addressable set is being created by the work being claimed, the
  share-of-addressable rung is UNAVAILABLE for that metric regardless of what the
  organization-wide rung list says, and the metric drops to the highest rung whose
  denominator was not chosen or built by the writer.
- Mechanism, the INDEPENDENCE TEST, run per metric, every run, not only on a
  resolution failure:
  1. Does the denominator column resolve and populate? A denominator that resolves
     and populates is NOT thereby independent. The old guard fired only on a
     resolution failure and this is exactly why it missed.
  2. Is the denominator a sum that includes the numerator? If so, compute the
     numerator's share of it.
  3. Where that share exceeds ADDRESSABLE_SELF_SHARE_CEILING, or where the
     denominator's own period-over-period movement tracks the numerator's within
     the deadband across the population, the set is SELF-BUILT. Strike the rung,
     say so, and name the figure that fired the test.
- Rule: a denominator whose value is being created by the writer's own team caps
  claim strength at BOUNDED for every figure computed against it, whatever rung
  the organization bound. Bounded means a number with its denominator population
  and no achievement wording.
- Prevents: the exact case. A launch computes share of a category that is the sum
  of the authorizations the writer's own team has just won, arrives at a share
  near one hundred percent by construction, and ships it at achievement strength
  with a WIN label. Every gate passes.
- Source: report 2 D5
- Applies: PLANNING, REVIEW, SCORECARD

### SD-CLM-31 No prior period for the population means no WIN and no MISS
- Rule: the win, miss and flat classifier requires a prior period for the
  population. Where the population is COLD, the classifier does not fire. No
  figure in that population carries a WIN label, a MISS label or a FLAT label.
- Rule: this is not the same as the floor rung turning the classifier off. It is
  an independent block, and it applies even where a rung that normally supports a
  label is in force, because the label asserts movement and there is nothing to
  have moved from.
- Rule: the state emitted instead is NO BASELINE. It is a real state, it is
  printed, and it is distinguishable from unpaired, from flat and from a
  suppressed label. A reader must be able to tell the difference between "this did
  not move", "this cannot be compared" and "this has nothing to be compared to
  yet".
- Rule: a plan attainment figure is exempt from this block and keeps its own three
  states, because attainment compares against a target rather than against a prior
  period, and a launch normally has a target. That is the point of the exemption.
- Prevents: a first-period number being labelled a win against nothing.
- Source: report 2 D5
- Applies: PLANNING, REVIEW, SCORECARD

### SD-CLM-32 What a person may honestly claim during a cold start, which is not nothing
- Rule: the cold-start rules forbid a specific class of claim. They do not forbid
  claiming. A launch is often the hardest work a person does all year and the
  method must be able to say so. Three kinds of claim survive a cold start intact,
  and the run surfaces all three rather than waiting to be asked:
  1. ABSOLUTE BUILD. What exists now that did not exist at the start of the
     period, stated as a count with its unit and its period. Doors opened,
     accounts activated, sites certified, programmes live, requirements
     implemented. It carries no comparison and needs none, because it is a
     statement of construction rather than of relative performance.
  2. SEQUENCING AGAINST PLAN. Attainment against a target set before the period by
     somebody other than the writer, in percent of target, with the three-state
     classifier that belongs to attainment. This is normally the strongest claim a
     cold population supports and it is a genuinely strong one: exceeding a
     launch plan is a real result.
  3. EXECUTION AGAINST A DENOMINATOR POPULATION. The floor rung, and it is a
     legitimate, gate-passing claim. So many of so many eligible, where the
     eligible set is named and bounded. It has a bound, a population and a scope.
- Rule: a cold-start run that produces none of these three has not done its job.
  Absence of a comparison is not absence of a finding.
- Rule: state the sequencing claim in the writer's own altitude and with a
  contribution verb where it ladders above their scope, exactly as any other claim.
  Nothing about a cold start relaxes the altitude ceiling.
- Rule: the front panel says, in plain words, that this population has no prior
  period, that no win or miss labels were computed for that reason, and which of
  the three claim kinds the figures below are.
- Source: report 2 D5
- Applies: PLANNING, REVIEW, SCORECARD

### SD-CLM-33 The rung decides the arithmetic, and a quantity that does not exist is never approximated
- Rule: which comparative quantities exist depends on the rung in force. A
  quantity that does not exist at a rung is NOT approximated by a neighbouring
  one. It is reported as not available for that metric, with the rung named.
- Mechanism, the rung table. This is the mechanism of SD-CLM-07 and it governs
  all three skills.

| Rung | Rate delta | Proportion delta | Label | Claim strength |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 share of addressable | Entity growth minus addressable-population growth, in percentage points of growth. | End share minus start share, in share points, where share is entity value divided by addressable value. | Computed from the rate delta against DEADBAND. | strong |
| 2 matched control | Treated growth minus control growth, in percentage points of growth. | Defined ONLY where treated and control together exhaust a bounded whole. Otherwise UNDEFINED and never emitted. | Computed from the rate delta against DEADBAND. | strong |
| 3 peer distribution | Own growth minus the peer median growth, in percentage points of growth. | Defined ONLY where the peer set is exhaustive of its parent. Otherwise UNDEFINED. | Computed against DEADBAND, carrying the peer count and the tie count. | qualified |
| 4 plan attainment | UNDEFINED. A plan has no growth rate. The quantity is ATTAINMENT, in percent of target, and ITS ARITHMETIC FOLLOWS THE TARGET'S SHAPE. | UNDEFINED. | Above target plus DEADBAND, below target minus DEADBAND, and ON PLAN inside the band. ON PLAN is the flat state at this rung. | qualified |
| 5 own prior run rate | Own period-over-period movement, in the measure's own unit or in percent, with NO external control subtracted. | UNDEFINED. | Computed against DEADBAND, emitted ONLY with the uncontrolled-movement warning. | qualified, with the warning |
| 6 bare denominator | UNDEFINED. | UNDEFINED. | UNPAIRED. The classifier does not fire. | bounded |

- Rule: the floor rung is reached only where no other bound rung resolves. A
  missing benchmark POPULATION is not by itself the floor: a peer set, a control
  group or a plan each supply a comparative quantity without one. See SD-CTR-24.
- Rule: under a cold start, SD-CLM-31 overrides the Label column for every rung
  except plan attainment, and SD-CLM-30 may strike rung 1 outright.
- **Rule: ATTAINMENT IS NOT ONE FORMULA. THE SHAPE OF THE TARGET DECIDES THE
  ARITHMETIC, AND THE SHAPE IS DECLARED BEFORE THE DIVISION IS DONE.** "Actual
  divided by target" is the arithmetic of ONE shape of target, and reading it as
  the definition of attainment is what makes the rung fail on ordinary targets. The
  shape decides because the two sides of the ratio must be the SAME QUANTITY: a
  target expressed as a movement is compared against a movement, a target expressed
  as a level against a level. Divide across two shapes and the same two numbers
  produce opposite labels, both arithmetically valid, in a permanent record. The
  band compounds it: a band calibrated for one shape treats almost everything under
  another shape as a miss.
- **Rule: WHERE ATTAINMENT IS UNDEFINED FOR THE TARGET AS STATED, RUNG 4 EMITS NO
  LABEL.** The classifier does not fire, the figure drops to the highest remaining
  rung, and the drop is NAMED beside the figure with the reason, per SD-CTR-24. A
  measure that falls off rung 4 still ships at rung 6 with its denominator
  population, which is a real claim, not a silence.
- **Rule: THE TARGET IS NEVER REWRITTEN TO MAKE THE DIVISION WORK.** Not restated as
  a level to escape a movement, not nudged off zero, not replaced by a proxy that
  divides cleanly. The target is what somebody set before the period; rewriting it
  makes the run the author of the plan it is scoring against, which is the same
  failure as choosing one's own denominator. The shape is reported as it was found,
  and the shape actually used is printed beside the figure.
- Rule: the enumerated shapes and the arithmetic each one takes live in the skill
  that computes them, so a skill may add a shape without amending doctrine. What
  lives here is the principle: same quantity on both sides, undefined means no
  label rather than a substitute, and the target is not edited.
- Prevents: two skills producing two different answers off the same configuration
  and the same data, one of them declaring every figure uncomparable and the other
  labelling the same figure a miss; and one target shape's arithmetic being applied
  to every other shape because it was the shape written down first.
- Source: report 1 D5; promoted from the review skill, unaltered
- Applies: PLANNING, REVIEW, SCORECARD
