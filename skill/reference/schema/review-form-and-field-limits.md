# GROUP 20: THE REVIEW FORM AND FIELD LIMITS

FORM_SECTIONS, RATING_SCALE, RATING_AXES, BEHAVIOUR_FRAMEWORK_ITEMS,
BEHAVIOUR_SCALE_VALUES, SELF_ASSESSMENT_PROMPTS and METRIC_SET hold CACHED COPIES
of published content, each behind a validity date. FORM_SPEC_SOURCE is the live
source and outranks every one of them. A cached copy past its date is refetched,
or used and marked stale. None of them is the authority a run scores against. See
standing rule S8.

| FIELD_BUDGET_SPLIT | OBJECTIVE_FIELD_IS_SINGLE is true and a field is written | Title 7 to 14 percent of the limit; description 15 to 21 percent; all measures the remainder, targeted at 80 percent of the limit overall. **THE ZERO ALLOCATION AND THE RELEASE, STATED AS ARITHMETIC SO TWO IMPLEMENTATIONS SPLIT A RELEASED BUDGET IDENTICALLY.** A component with NO SOURCE in the material is allocated ZERO. Its allocation is RELEASED to the remaining components IN PROPORTION TO THEIR OWN ALLOCATIONS: each remaining component's new allocation is its old allocation plus the released amount times its old allocation divided by the SUM of the old allocations of the components that remain. Rounding is DOWN to whole characters, and any remainder from rounding goes to the component with the largest old allocation, so the arithmetic is deterministic and the total is unchanged. **A COMPONENT IS NEVER WRITTEN TO FILL A BUDGET.** Where the source objective carries no description, the field holds the title and the measures, the description takes zero, and the release is recorded in the method section naming the component and the characters released. Where only one component has a source, it takes the whole budget by the same arithmetic. | The internal split of the objective field was not set, so a standing proportional budget was used.
