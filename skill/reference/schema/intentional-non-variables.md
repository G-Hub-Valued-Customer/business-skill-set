# APPENDIX A3: INTENTIONAL NON-VARIABLES

Two classes of name in this bundle are written in upper case and are NOT
variables. Neither is bindable, neither has a default or a degradation notice,
and neither should ever be reported by an automated sweep as a variable defined
nowhere. They are recorded here so the distinction is documented rather than
tribal knowledge.

## A3.1 The counterfactual rung names

SHARE_OF_ADDRESSABLE, MATCHED_CONTROL, PEER_DISTRIBUTION, PLAN_ATTAINMENT,
OWN_PRIOR_RUN_RATE, BARE_DENOMINATOR.

Owned by Group 6 of this file, which states that the six rungs of
COUNTERFACTUAL_LADDER are a fixed structure and are not configurable. What IS
bound is which rungs a business has available, which rung each metric uses, how
strongly each rung lets a claim be worded, and whether availability is scoped to
a population. The rung NAMES are fixed vocabulary, in the same way that FIXED and
FLOW are fixed vocabulary for a population mode.

## A3.2 The capability names

FILE_READ, FILE_WRITE, CODE_EXECUTION, SPREADSHEET_WRITE, SPREADSHEET_INSPECT,
DOCUMENT_WRITE, DOCUMENT_INSPECT, DOCUMENT_EXTRACT, SHELL, SYSTEM_OF_RECORD, MAIL,
DIRECTORY, DOC_STORE, CHAT_AND_CALENDAR, WEB, MEMORY, INTERACTIVE.

**DOCUMENT_WRITE AND DOCUMENT_INSPECT ARE ON THIS LIST AND WERE MISSING FROM IT.**
They are the container capabilities of the skill whose OUTPUT_MEDIUM is a document,
exactly as the two spreadsheet capabilities are for a skill whose medium is a
workbook, and while they were absent the sweep in A3.3 reported one skill's entire
deliverable path as depending on two variables defined nowhere. This roster is
copied from reference/capability-probe.md 1.3 and must be re-copied whole whenever that probe
order changes; a roster maintained by adding one name at a time is how these two
went missing.

Owned by reference/capability-probe.md 1.3, which is the authority on the roster and on the
probe order. They name things a run DISCOVERS about its environment, not things an
organization DECLARES about itself, which is why they are not in this schema.
Their four states, PRESENT, ABSENT, DEGRADED and UNKNOWN, are fixed vocabulary for
the same reason.

## A3.3 The test a sweep should apply

An upper-case token is a schema variable only if it appears in the NAME column of a
table in Groups 1 through 25 of this file. Everything else in upper case is one of:
a rung name from A3.1; a capability name from A3.2; a fixed enumerated value such
as FIXED, FLOW, IGNITION, DEFERRED, OPTIONAL, CONDITIONAL, PASS, GAP,
UNASSESSABLE, NOT REQUIRED, RETIRED, WIN, MISS, FLAT, UNPAIRED, NO BASELINE,
PRESENT, ABSENT, DEGRADED, UNKNOWN, stop_outright or ask_one_question_then_stop; one of the LEARNING-LOG ENUMERATIONS in reference/field-resolution.md Part 7, which are UNRESOLVED, ASKED, VALUE_MATCH, REJECTED, AMBIGUOUS and ADOPTED_PROVISIONAL for the event, and USER, VALUE_CHECK, RESOLVER and NOBODY for who decided it;
one of the SEVEN SHAPE TOKENS named by the RIGHT_ALIGN_COLUMNS membership rule,
which are COUNT_OR_MEASURE, RATE_OR_PROPORTION, DATE, IDENTIFIER_OR_CODE,
STATUS_OR_CATEGORY, BOOLEAN_LIKE and FREE_TEXT; one of the MEMBER CODES of
REASON_CODE_SET or CLASSIFICATION_LABELS, whose members are enumerated in their own
SECTION A1 rows and are values rather than variables; a NAMING-CONVENTION token such as UPPER_SNAKE_CASE, which names the shape of a variable name rather than naming a variable; a COLUMN NAME or a LOG EVENT VALUE belonging to a record schema in reference/field-resolution.md, such as VALUE_CHECK, which is a column of the seed dictionary, or VALUE_MATCH, which is one permitted value of the resolution log's event field; a doctrine id; or emphasis. A
sweep that does not carry this test will rediscover all of them as defects on every
pass, and the shape tokens and the reason codes are exactly what it rediscovered
last time.

# APPENDIX A4: THE NOT-WORKABLE MATCH RULE, VERIFIED RATHER THAN ASSERTED

SD-POP-29 requires that where a rule's notice names example inputs, those exact
inputs are RUN and their results printed. An earlier version of this rule carried a
notice claiming three traps had been verified and failed one of them, which spends
a reader's trust to hide a defect. So this table is generated by parsing the seed
set and the marker list out of THEIR SECTION A1 ROWS, which are the one home of
both lists, and running the rule on each string,
not by reading them and forming an opinion.

**THE SIX REQUIRED STRINGS:**

| Status string | Result | Required | Matched by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Non-Renew Pending | EXCLUDE | EXCLUDE | the phrase "non renew" |
| Active With Renewal Due | KEEP | KEEP | nothing; "withdrawn" is one token and does not equal "with" |
| Renewal Quoted | KEEP | KEEP | nothing; no bare word for a quote is in the seed set |
| Renewal Pending | KEEP | KEEP | nothing; "pending cancellation" needs both tokens adjacent |
| Up For Renewal | KEEP | KEEP | nothing |
| Active | KEEP | KEEP | nothing |

**THE REGRESSION SET, so the rule is not verified only on the six it was written
for.** Twenty-eight further strings, all correct: EXCLUDE for prospect, withdrawn,
do not renew, non-renewal, in run-off, notice given, pending cancellation,
suspended, on hold, cancelled, lost, inactive, terminated, trial, lead. KEEP for
client - active, new business, won, renewal application received, active client,
in force, current, renewal won, open, renewal in progress, quote requested, active
trial, open lead. Thirty-four cases, zero failures.

The last two pairs are the single-token override doing its job and being seen to:
a bare trial or a bare lead is excluded, while an active trial and an open lead are
KEPT with the refusal printed, because a compound status naming a live relationship
needs the exit stated as a phrase rather than implied by one word.
