WHY THE CHANGES
---------------
1. The old first screen led with a feature table and "plain text, no install".
   The strongest fact in the whole repo, that built output files are sitting in
   sample-output/ and can be judged in thirty seconds, was below the fold in the
   Try It section. It is now the second thing a reader sees.

2. The seventeen-round defect burn-down was buried under Verification, two thirds
   of the way down. For a technical reader that table is the single most credible
   thing in the project, so it is now above the fold. The duplicate copy in the
   Verification section was replaced with a pointer so nothing reads twice.

3. The two sharpest trap examples (the +2.72 percent premium growth graded a MISS,
   and the lines-of-business objective that refused to grade itself) were moved up
   with the table. They are what make the testing claim checkable rather than
   assertable, and they are the best thirty seconds of reading in the repo.

4. Everything from "Try it" onward is unchanged. The ideas section, the portability
   section, the honest limits and the license note were already right.
