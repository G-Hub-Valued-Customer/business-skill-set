# Fairhaven Risk Partners: account servicing standard

Version 4.2, effective 2026-07-01. Owner: Renata Alvi, Director of Operations.

Every active account is expected to meet all eight requirements below. Producers
are scored on the eight at each renewal. Requirements 7 and 8 are set outside the
agency and are reproduced here because they are scored the same way.

| # | Requirement | What counts as met | Who sets it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Renewal meeting booked 60 days out | A calendar entry 60 or more days before expiration | The agency |
| 2 | Loss runs ordered | Current loss runs on file, dated within 90 days | The agency |
| 3 | Coverage checklist completed | The checklist signed and dated by the producer | The agency |
| 4 | Certificate backlog clear | No open certificate request older than 5 business days | The agency |
| 5 | Exposure re-verified | Payroll, receipts or values confirmed with the client this term | The agency |
| 6 | Written proposal delivered | A dated proposal document on file | The agency |
| 7 | Flood zone determination on file | A determination dated within the policy term | Federal requirement |
| 8 | Carrier appetite confirmation | Written carrier confirmation the class is in appetite | Carrier, per program |

## A note on requirement 8

Appetite confirmation is issued by the carrier's underwriting desk. A producer
requests it; the carrier grants or refuses it. Where a carrier has closed a class
agency-wide, no producer action can obtain it.
