# Fairhaven Risk Partners: October 2026 production priorities

Issued 2026-09-25 by Renata Alvi, Director of Operations.

## The three priorities for October

1. **Round out single-line accounts.** Any account writing one line only is a
   retention risk and a growth opportunity. Quote at least one additional line.
2. **Get ahead of the carrier rate filings.** Meridian Mutual and Harborline both
   filed increases effective 1 November. Any account renewing on or after that
   date should be re-marketed before the client sees the number.
3. **Close the certificate backlog.** Accounts carrying open service items are
   the accounts that leave. Clear them before the renewal conversation, not after.

## Lines of business, in the order the agency wants them grown

Workers Comp, Commercial Property, General Liability, Commercial Auto, Cyber,
Professional Liability, Umbrella, Inland Marine.

## What is not a priority this month

New business prospecting. October is a renewal month. Prospect records stay in
the system and out of the plan.
