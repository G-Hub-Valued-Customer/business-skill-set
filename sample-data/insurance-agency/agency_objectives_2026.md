# Fairhaven Risk Partners: 2026 agency objectives

Published 2026-01-08 by Renata Alvi, Director of Operations, for the Principal.

## The four agency objectives for 2026

1. **Grow written premium by 6 percent.** Measured on written premium, full year
   against full year.
2. **Hold retention at or above 88 percent.** Measured on accounts, not premium.
3. **Round out the book to 2.0 lines per account.** Single-line accounts are the
   retention risk and the growth opportunity.
4. **Clear the certificate backlog.** No account should carry an open service item
   into its renewal conversation.

## A note on the market this year

Meridian Mutual, Harborline and Statewide Indemnity all filed rate increases
taking effect through 2026. Written premium will rise on renewal alone, without
any producer action. Growth against objective 1 should be read against that.
